FAQ

❓ Questions

Is this a pump and dump?

No. $BOSS is being built as a long-running game project: two games already playable, new maps and skins every round, and every change published in the public build log. The dev wallet follows a public 1%-per-day sell limit. None of that is a promise about price: it's a meme coin and can go to zero.

What's the sandbox?

An original first-person build-and-mine world (beta). It's single-player practice right now. The SOL shards you mine are in-game items with no cash value. Multiplayer and its own leaderboard are on the plan.

Is the game really free?

Yes. Practice mode runs in any browser with no wallet. Ranked mode only needs the free Phantom wallet: signing in is free and is not a transaction. There's nothing to buy.

Do I need to hold $BOSS to play or win?

No. Holding, buying or burning $BOSS never affects whether you can play, your tries, your score or whether you win. Holders of 100,000+ $BOSS only get cosmetic perks (skins, a 👑 badge, early access to new practice maps).

How are prizes paid?

Each round's pot is 20% of the pump.fun creator fees actually received during that round. The top 5 split it 40 / 25 / 15 / 12 / 8%. If the pot is under 0.05 SOL it rolls over to the next round. Nothing is pre-funded or guaranteed. Full details are in the official rules.

How do you stop cheating?

Each ranked run is signed by your wallet, and the server replays the exact run from its seed and your inputs before the score counts. Each wallet gets 3 tries per round, and linked wallets count as one person for prizes.

Can I play on my iPhone?

Practice works in Safari or any phone browser: tap “Open fullscreen”. Ranked needs the Phantom app: open this site inside Phantom's built-in browser (the site shows an “Open in Phantom” button on phones).

When does the Troglodyte Fudder claim close?

At the end of Round 2: Wed Oct 7, 9 PM ET. Wallets holding 100,000+ $BOSS can claim it free from the Skins menu in the ranked game until then. After that it can't be claimed.

Where's the official contract address?

Only on this site and on @TheBossCoin_SOL. Always compare the full address. Never share your wallet's recovery phrase with anyone.

Will $BOSS make me money?

No promises, ever. $BOSS is a joke meme coin with no intrinsic value, no utility and no expectation of profit. Meme coins are extremely risky. Nothing on this site is financial advice.
