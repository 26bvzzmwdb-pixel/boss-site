No purchase, payment, token holding, or blockchain transaction is needed to play or to win. Buying, holding or burning $BOSS does not improve your chances, your tries or your score. Playing the game is free. Rewards are determined only by skill, as measured by the verified game score.

3. Eligibility

  • Open to individuals who are at least 18 years old, or the age of majority where they live if that is older [TBD: 18+ or 21+].
  • Void where prohibited or restricted by law. Not open to residents of [TBD: excluded jurisdictions], or to anyone in a country or on a list subject to sanctions (for example OFAC lists).

  • One person, one entry wallet. Using multiple wallets, bots, scripts, or automation to get extra tries or scores is not allowed and leads to disqualification.
  • One prize per person per round. Wallets that appear to belong to the same person are treated as one entrant ("linked wallets"), for example wallets funded from the same source wallet, played from the same device, or otherwise linked by the Sponsor's review. Only the best-ranked score of a linked group can win; the next eligible entrant moves up.
  • Lifetime cap: one person (including linked wallets) can receive at most US$200 worth of rewards in total across all rounds [TBD: confirm amount].

4. How to play (enter)

  • Open the ranked game at the official link posted on X and connect a Solana wallet (Phantom). Connecting is free and sends no transaction.
  • Each wallet gets 3 ranked tries per round. Tries reset when a new round starts. The limit is enforced by the game server.
  • At the end of a run you sign a free, off-chain message with your wallet to submit your score. Signing costs nothing and moves no funds.
  • Rounds last 3 days and run back to back. Round 1 runs from Thu Oct 1, 2026, 9:00 PM ET (01:00 UTC Oct 2) to Sun Oct 4, 2026, 9:00 PM ET; each next round starts when the previous one ends. Rewards for any round apply only once the official rules have been posted on X [TBD: confirm]. A live countdown is shown in the game and on the website.

5. Score verification

  • Every ranked run uses a server-issued session and random seed. The server re-plays your recorded inputs and only accepts the score if the replay gives the same result.
  • The server also rejects runs that are faster than real time, have impossible score rates or input rates, reuse or alter a signed submission, or don't match the wallet's signature. Rate limits apply.
  • Only verified scores count. Your best verified score in the round is your leaderboard score.
  • The Sponsor may review the top scores and disqualify any entry it reasonably believes involved cheating, exploits, bots, multiple wallets, or tampering. Decisions on verification are final [TBD: dispute process].
  • Ties: the score submitted first ranks higher.

6. Rewards and cap

  • The top 10 verified scores of a round are eligible for rewards paid in SOL.
  • Rewards are funded only from pump.fun creator trading fees the Sponsor actually received. Nothing is pre-funded. There is no minimum and no guaranteed amount. If fees are low, the round's rewards are reduced or skipped, and this is announced on X.
  • Fees received are split 50% prize pot, 40% reserve and 10% operations. The reserve and operations shares never pay rewards. Each round pays at most 50% of the prize pot, and never more than the round cap for the current tier:
    market cap under $100K: round cap $100, max $20 per winner · $100K–500K: $400 / $60 · $500K–2M: $1,200 / $180 · over $2M: $3,000 / $450.
    The tier uses the lower of the round's average and closing market cap. The pool can grow as the coin grows, and shrinks if it falls. Caps are maximums, not promises.
  • The round pool is split by rank: #1 25%, #2 18%, #3 13%, #4 10%, #5 8%, #6 7%, #7 6%, #8 5%, #9 4%, #10 4%. Each share is capped at the per-winner cap, valued at the SOL/USD price when the payout is sent. Amounts that aren't paid stay in the pot for later rounds. Shares under US$1 aren't sent.
  • Rewards are not transferable and can't be exchanged for cash or other prizes. Winners are responsible for any taxes. The Sponsor may request information needed for legal or tax compliance [TBD: KYC threshold].
